Both options can work. The right one depends on your budget, timeline and appetite for risk.

## Ready-to-move flat
- **You see what you buy.** Size, light, view, finishing and neighbours are real.
- **No construction delay.** You can move in after registration and handover.
- **Established area.** Roads and services already exist.
- **Often costs more per square foot** than an under-construction flat in a newer area.

## Under-construction flat
- **Lower entry price and staged payments** in many projects.
- **Delay risk.** Possession can slip. Check the builder's track record and RERA registration.
- **You pay while you wait,** often while also paying rent or EMI.
- **Finish and layout** are only known when the work is done.

## Which suits you?
- Need to move soon, or want certainty → **ready-to-move**.
- Can wait and want a newer building → **under construction**, with a reputable builder.
