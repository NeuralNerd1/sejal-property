Citylight sits in the main city area of Surat, Gujarat. If you are comparing localities for a home purchase, this guide lists what to evaluate. Verify distances and travel times on a map for your own routine.

## Location and connectivity
Citylight is close to several of Surat's well-known neighbourhoods, including Athwa, Parle Point and Vesu. Being in the main city means shorter trips for work, school and errands than the outer areas.

## Daily conveniences to check
- Schools and colleges within your preferred travel time
- Hospitals and clinics
- Grocery, vegetable and general markets
- Banks, ATMs and pharmacies
- Restaurants, cafés and places of worship

## Things to check on a visit
1. **Water supply** and pressure at different times of day.
2. **Parking** for residents and visitors.
3. **Traffic and noise** during morning and evening peaks.
4. **Building condition** – lifts, staircases, terrace, seepage marks.
5. **Neighbours and society management** – a short chat tells you a lot.

## Who it suits
Families and professionals who want a central address and a ready-to-use home, without a long commute.
