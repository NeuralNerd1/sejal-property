A top-floor flat has its own strengths and weak points. Here is a fair look before you decide.

## Advantages
- **More light and air.** Fewer buildings block the view, and a flat open on several sides is usually brighter and better ventilated.
- **No neighbours above.** No noise from footsteps overhead.
- **Quieter and more privacy** than lower floors.
- **Terrace access** in many buildings.

## Drawbacks
- **Heat.** The roof absorbs sun, so rooms below can get warmer in summer. Check roof insulation or waterproofing.
- **Leakage risk.** Roofs age. Look for damp patches, cracks and paint peeling on the ceiling.
- **Lift dependence.** Check that the lift works reliably, and that the building has backup power for it.
- **Water pressure.** Top floors can see lower pressure if tanks and pumps are weak.

## What to check on a visit
1. Ceilings in every room for stains.
2. Whether the terrace has been waterproofed and who maintains it.
3. Cross-ventilation: open the windows and feel the airflow.
4. Water pressure in the bathrooms and kitchen.
