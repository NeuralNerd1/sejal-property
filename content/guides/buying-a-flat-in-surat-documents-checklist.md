Before you pay a token amount for a flat in Surat, verify the paperwork. This checklist covers what buyers commonly ask to see. It is general information, not legal advice – have a property lawyer review the documents.

## Ownership and title
- **Sale deed / title deed** of the current owner and the chain of earlier agreements.
- Land and ownership records as applicable (for example Index-II) to confirm ownership.
- **Allotment letter or society share certificate**, if the flat is in a cooperative housing society.

## Approvals
- **Approved building plan** and **building use (BU) permission**.
- **Society NOC** for the sale, where required.

## Dues and receipts
- **Property tax receipts** from Surat Municipal Corporation (SMC).
- **Electricity, water and gas bills** – confirm there are no pending dues.
- **Society maintenance receipts** and any pending transfer fees.

## Loan and encumbrance
- Confirm there is **no existing mortgage**. If there is one, ask for the bank's release letter at closing.
- Obtain an **encumbrance / search report** through your lawyer.

## At closing
- Agreement to sale, then **sale deed registration** at the sub-registrar office. See [how registration works](/guides/property-registration-process-in-surat-gujarat/).
- Keep copies of the **stamp duty and registration receipts**.
