A furnished flat saves the cost and time of interiors. Before you buy one in Surat, be clear about what is included and what condition it is in.

## What "fully furnished" commonly includes
- Beds and mattresses, wardrobes and storage
- Dining table and chairs, sofa and tables
- Curtains and light fittings, ceiling fans
- Modular kitchen with cabinets and a water purifier
- Air conditioners in some rooms

## Inspection checklist
1. **Wardrobes and cabinets** – hinges, drawers, locks and water damage.
2. **Electricals** – switches, sockets, fans, geyser and AC working condition.
3. **Plumbing** – taps, drains and any signs of seepage in bathrooms and utility area.
4. **Walls and ceilings** – damp patches or cracks.
5. **Windows and grills** – smooth operation and secure fitting.

## Write the inclusions into the agreement
Appliances and furniture are not part of the property by default. List every item you expect to stay – air conditioners, geyser, fans, wardrobes, the dining set – in the agreement to sale, so there is no confusion at handover.

## Factor the value in
Furniture and fittings reduce your set-up cost, but they age. Judge the price on the flat first and treat furnishings as added convenience.
