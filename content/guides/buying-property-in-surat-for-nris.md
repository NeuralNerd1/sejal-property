Many NRIs buy flats in Surat for family use or as an investment. Rules change, so treat this as a general outline and check current regulations with a chartered accountant or lawyer.

## Typical steps
1. **Shortlist the flat** – use a trusted relative, friend or agent to visit it, or ask the owner for a video walkthrough.
2. **Check the documents** – use the [documents checklist](/guides/buying-a-flat-in-surat-documents-checklist/) and have a lawyer in Surat verify the title.
3. **Plan the payment route** – payments are normally made through banking channels from an NRI account. Confirm the current requirements with your bank.
4. **Sign the agreement** and pay stamp duty and registration charges. See the [registration process](/guides/property-registration-process-in-surat-gujarat/).
5. **Registration in person or via power of attorney** – if you cannot travel, a power of attorney may be used, drawn up correctly and attested as required.
6. **Hand over and manage** – arrange maintenance, bills and tenant or caretaker arrangements.

## Points to confirm with a professional
- Current foreign-exchange rules on buying residential property and on sending sale proceeds abroad
- Tax implications in India and in your country of residence
- How to draft and attest a power of attorney
