This is a general overview of how a flat purchase is formalised in Surat. Rules and rates change, so confirm current details with the Gujarat registration department or a property lawyer.

## 1. Agreement to sale
After you and the seller agree on the price, sign an agreement to sale. It states the price, payment schedule, included fittings and furniture, and a handover date.

## 2. Pay stamp duty and registration fee
Stamp duty and registration charges are payable on the **higher of the sale price or the government's jantri (guideline) value**. Check the current rates and the jantri value for the area before budgeting, as these are revised from time to time.

## 3. Sale deed registration
Both buyer and seller, with two witnesses, visit the **sub-registrar office** with ID proofs and documents. The sale deed is signed, biometrics are captured and the deed is registered.

## 4. Update records
After registration, update:
- **Society records** and share certificate transfer
- **Property tax** records with Surat Municipal Corporation
- **Electricity, gas and water** connections in your name

## 5. Handover
Take possession against the agreed inventory, check fittings, and collect all original documents.
