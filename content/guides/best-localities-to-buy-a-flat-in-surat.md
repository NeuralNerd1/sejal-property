Surat has many popular residential areas, including Athwa, Vesu, Adajan, Piplod, Pal and Althan. Rather than chasing a "best" locality, compare them against **your own priorities**.

## Decide what matters most
- **Commute** – distance to your workplace or business.
- **Schools and hospitals** for your family.
- **Budget** – prices vary widely between areas and buildings.
- **Newer vs established** – developing areas offer newer buildings; established areas offer finished surroundings.
- **Future plans** – check planned roads or metro routes with the Surat Municipal Corporation.

## Compare on the ground
1. Visit each area at morning and evening peak hours.
2. Time the trip from the flat to your regular destinations.
3. Check water supply, parking and flooding history with residents.
4. Walk to the nearest market, school and clinic.
