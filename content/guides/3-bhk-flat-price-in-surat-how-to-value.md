The price of a **3 BHK flat in Surat** depends mainly on the locality, the size, the floor, the age of the building and what is included. Instead of relying on one number, build your own estimate.

## Step 1: Compare similar flats
Look at at least five flats with a similar size, age and locality. Note the **price per square foot on carpet area** so the comparison is fair. See [carpet area vs super built-up area](/guides/carpet-area-vs-super-built-up-area-explained/).

## Step 2: Check the jantri value
The government's jantri (guideline) rate for the area sets the minimum value for stamp duty. A price far below the jantri value is unusual. Confirm the current rate with the sub-registrar office.

## Step 3: Adjust for the flat itself
- **Floor and view** – top floors with light and ventilation can be valued differently from lower floors. See [top-floor pros and cons](/guides/top-floor-flat-pros-and-cons/).
- **Age and condition** – repairs and seepage reduce value.
- **Furnishing** – a furnished flat saves you interiors cost, but furniture ages. Judge the flat first.
- **Amenities** – lift, security, CCTV and parking add convenience.

## Step 4: Budget the extras
Add stamp duty, registration, brokerage (if any), society transfer fees and any repairs. See the [registration process](/guides/property-registration-process-in-surat-gujarat/).
